设计说明全文 / FULL DESIGN NOTES

中文全译 / CHINESE TRANSLATION

Ziying Liang的系列概念来自那些通过穿着男装而获得女性气质的电影角色。其方法包括解构典型男性服装，将其与作为女性化基础服装的芭蕾短裙结合，并把带有男性气质的条纹面料与柔软薄纱相配，以塑造雌雄同体的特质。Liang研究经典男装的着装规范与女式芭蕾短裙的构造技法，沿着男装内部的女性曲线继续延展，尝试建立一种独特的雌雄同体美学。

英文原文 / ENGLISH ORIGINAL

The concept for Ziying Liang’s collection comes from film characters who gain femininity by wearing menswear. This involves deconstructing typical male garments and integrating them with the tutu (as a feminine foundational garment), and combining masculine striped fabrics with soft tulle fabrics to shape an androgynous quality. Liang studied the dressing norms of classic menswear and the construction techniques of women’s tutus, extending along the feminine curves within menswear to attempt to create a unique androgynous aesthetic.
